## Use and coverage

The application uses these records only for deterministic, illustrative proximity scoring. It does not treat them as a real-time feed or as evidence of personal safety.

Coverage means the length of a road segment within 200 metres of at least one accepted record. Partial or uncovered routes have unknown risk and receive no safest label. See `source_metadata.json` for machine-readable metadata.
